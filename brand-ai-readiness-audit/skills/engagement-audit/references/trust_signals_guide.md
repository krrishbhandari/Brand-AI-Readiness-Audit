# Trust Signals, Authority Anchors & Conversion Hierarchy

This reference defines the required authority markers and conversion pathways necessary to retain visitors and build trust.

---

## 1. Essential Trust & Authority Anchors

Visitors referred from AI engines require verification signals to confirm credibility:

| Trust Category | Required Elements | Audit Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct Contact** | Clear support email (`support@domain.com`), telephone number, or physical corporate address. | **High** |
| **Legal & Privacy** | Prominent links to Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and GDPR/CCPA compliance notices. | **High** |
| **Security & Certifications** | Verification of SSL/HTTPS, SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or industry trust badges. | **Medium** |
| **Social Proof & Verification** | Case studies, customer logos, verifiable testimonials with real names and titles. | **Medium** |

---

## 2. Navigation & Context Retention

- **Clear Heading Tree**: Strict progression (`H1` -> `H2` -> `H3`) allowing users seeking answers to specific sub-questions to jump directly to the relevant section.
- **Persistent Navigation & Breadcrumbs**: Allows users landing on deep documentation or product sub-pages to navigate upward to category or home views without hitting dead ends.
- **Conversion Pathways (CTAs)**: Unambiguous primary Call-to-Action button (e.g. *"Start Free Trial"*, *"View Live Demo"*, *"Read Documentation"*) without intrusive full-screen popups or blocking overlays.
